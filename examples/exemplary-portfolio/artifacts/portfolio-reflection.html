Document contents

LEARN

I learned to separate symptoms from cause, to record expected versus observed behavior, and to recognize when change authority matters.

PRACTICE

My first support records described the action but missed the test that confirmed success. A peer review revealed the evidence gap.

OPERATE

In a simulated support workflow I recorded the user's impact, gathered non-sensitive evidence, performed authorized checks, and closed only after verification.

QUALIFY

I can now explain a troubleshooting choice, show a reviewed KB procedure, walk through a redacted validation report, and describe how feedback improved the work.

BEFORE AND AFTER

Before: 'Changed DNS and fixed problem.' After: 'Gateway reachable, lookup failed, approved DNS restored, lookup and application both revalidated.'

NEXT TARGET

Improve accessibility for the KB reader, reduce ambiguity in decision trees, and collect stronger peer review evidence.
