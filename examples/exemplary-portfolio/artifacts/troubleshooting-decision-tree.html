Document contents

Start condition

Report: 'I cannot open the training website.' Capture URL, exact message, affected users, time, and device state.

Decision A

Can other known websites open? YES: isolate the target site. NO: check link, IP configuration, gateway, and broader network.

Decision B

Can the approved target IP be reached? YES and name fails: investigate DNS path. NO: investigate route, service, or filtering with approved diagnostics.

Decision C

Does a different approved device reproduce the symptom? YES: escalate as possible shared service issue. NO: continue authorized local device investigation.

Stop conditions

Unknown authorization, suspected security incident, change needed outside scope, or multiple impacted users: stop and escalate.

Close condition

Reproduce initial failure, document hypothesis and test, perform approved fix, retest, confirm with user, and provide rollback notes.

Evidence trail

Record observed signals and precise branch selected. Decision branches are hypotheses until supported by testing.
