Article metadata

Title: Diagnose DNS name-resolution failures. Category: Networking / Client Connectivity. Audience: entry-level service desk technicians. Owner: CIT-205 learning KB.

Symptoms

Device can communicate with an approved IP endpoint but cannot open a training website by hostname. Record exact error, time, and whether issue affects one or many users.

Safety & prerequisites

Authorized workstation and terminal. Do not collect passwords. Do not alter production DNS or firewall settings without change approval.

Procedure 1

Record affected application and scope. Check local link status and assigned address; distinguish wired/Wi-Fi state.

Procedure 2

Test gateway connectivity. Test approved IP endpoint. Perform a DNS lookup of an approved training hostname; capture sanitized outputs.

Procedure 3

Compare observed resolver configuration against published approved baseline. If unauthorized or broad outage, stop and escalate.

Procedure 4

Only with approval, restore authorized configuration, keep rollback record, repeat all tests, and obtain user confirmation.

Expected results

Working IP path plus failed hostname lookup suggests DNS investigation; simultaneous failure suggests wider connectivity issue; one-host failure suggests host-specific issue.

Escalation & rollback

Escalate multiple impacted devices, inconsistent authoritative DNS, unauthorized settings, or unsuccessful remediation. Restore original approved values on rollback.
