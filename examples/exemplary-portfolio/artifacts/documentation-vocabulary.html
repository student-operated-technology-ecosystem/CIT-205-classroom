Document contents

Purpose

Demonstrate support terminology through contextual statements rather than a disconnected memorization list.

Symptom

User-observable behavior: 'The browser reports that the training hostname cannot be found.'

Scope

Number of impacted people/assets: 'The first report involves one workstation; other devices remain unverified.'

Baseline

Documented expected state: 'The approved resolver should return the published training host.'

Reproduction

Repeatable series: 'The same lookup fails on two runs under the recorded conditions.'

Evidence

Observable record: 'The sanitized lookup log includes command, time, and resulting error.'

Workaround

Temporary restoration: 'A permitted alternative endpoint permits work to continue, but does not resolve root cause.'

Root cause

A confirmed mechanism supported by evidence, not a guess. If not confirmed, explicitly label it unconfirmed.

Escalation

Transfer when authority or expertise is exceeded, carrying the symptom, scope, evidence, and attempted steps.

Validation

Confirming the change delivered the expected result. Rollback | Defined way to restore prior approved state.
