Document contents

Mission

Standardize workstation readiness validation within a fictional instructional service desk.

Problem

Inconsistent handoffs mean technicians cannot prove an endpoint meets the same baseline; work may be duplicated.

Outcome

A versioned checklist, validation record template, escalation rules, and a reviewer acceptance checklist.

Stakeholders

CIT-205 learners, instructor/reviewer, future support technicians. No real production authorization is asserted.

Method

Research existing baselines, draft checklist, pilot three training devices using two independent reviewers, record disagreements, revise, seek approval.

Acceptance

Two technicians independently obtain matching pass/flag decisions for three training devices; artifacts include evidence and privacy checks.

Dependencies

Training asset access, approved baseline, review time. Risks: oversharing credentials, unauthorized changes, inconsistent scoring.

Mitigation

Sanitization requirements, sign-off before changes, explicit expected vs actual, and peer-review checkpoint.
