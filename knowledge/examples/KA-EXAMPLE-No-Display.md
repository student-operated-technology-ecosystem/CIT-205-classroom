# EXAMPLE — Workstation Powers On but No Display

**Status:** Classroom Accepted — fictional teaching example  
**Applies to:** A fictional desktop workstation with an external monitor

## Problem and Symptoms

The user reports that the computer seems to power on, but the monitor is blank. The responsible component has not been confirmed.

## Questions to Ask

- What lights, sounds, or messages do you observe?
- Did the display work previously?
- Did anything change or move recently?
- Can the monitor display its own menu?

## Before You Begin

Obtain permission before moving equipment or interrupting work. Record the starting condition.

## Troubleshooting Steps

1. Confirm that the monitor has power and observe its power indicator.
2. Confirm that the selected monitor input matches the connected cable.
3. Check accessible connections without forcing, opening, or disassembling equipment.
4. Before considering a restart, ask about unsaved work and obtain permission. If interruption is not authorized or work cannot be saved, stop and escalate.
5. If a restart is appropriate, record what happens afterward.

## Likely Resolution

A disconnected monitor power cable or incorrect input could explain the scenario, but neither should be reported as confirmed without evidence.

## Verification

Confirm that the expected image appears and the user can see the sign-in screen or desktop. Record the action that produced the result.

## Stop and Escalate

Stop if equipment appears damaged, a step requires elevated permission, unsaved work is at risk, or the safe checks do not isolate the problem. Record lights, messages, connections, tests, and results for the next technician.
