## Article Status

- **Student Draft** — proposed work; not accepted.
- **In Review** — being evaluated through a pull request.
- **Classroom Accepted** — accepted for classroom learning.
- **Superseded** — replaced but retained in history.

“Classroom Accepted” does not authorize work on production or SOTE infrastructure.

## Find, Use, Improve

1. Find the article through this index.
2. Read its scope, assumptions, and status.
3. Decide whether it applies to the scenario.
4. Follow only steps inside your assignment and authority.
5. Record missing, unclear, or unsafe guidance.
6. Propose a specific improvement through peer review.
